## Product Hunt assets

Maker comment:

Hi Product Hunt,

I built BatchCutout because many small ecommerce sellers do not need a full design suite for every product-photo task. They need a fast way to remove backgrounds from a batch, check the output, and export transparent PNGs or a ZIP.

The product is browser-based: images are processed locally in the browser, not uploaded to BatchCutout servers.

Current limits:

- 2 images free for testing.
- Pro supports 100 images per batch.
- 2,000 images per month.
- PNG and ZIP export.

I would especially like feedback from Shopify, WooCommerce, Etsy, eBay and Amazon sellers who prepare repeated product batches.

## Directory checklist

Before submitting:

- Confirm homepage and pricing work on mobile.
- Confirm `https://batchcutout.com/customer-results/` is live.
- Prepare one 1200x630 social image from the current before/after asset.
- Prepare one short screen recording of upload -> remove background -> ZIP export.
- Make sure support email is monitored on launch day.

On launch day:

- Submit with UTM links for the directory.
- Reply manually to comments.
- Check `/admin` for uploads, leads, Pro clicks and checkouts every few hours.
- Do not raise ad spend until launch traffic shows uploads or plan clicks.
